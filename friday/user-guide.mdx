## Feature overview

Friday is an AI agent built into Neo. You ask in plain language, and Friday reads your tasks, documents, and connected systems, takes action, and leaves the output as real, editable work rather than as chat text. The aim is to cut the time you spend on routine coordination - writing updates, reformatting documents, triaging tasks, and hunting for context across tools - by letting an assistant that already knows your workspace do it with you.

**Where it lives:** Friday is one of the Neo products, alongside Tasket (the work platform) and Studio (the shared knowledge space). Open it from the product menu at the top left (**Switch product**). The Friday home has four tabs - **My Sessions**, **Team Sessions**, **Skills**, and **Connectors**. You can also start a session from inside a Tasket task, from the task’s **Friday** tab.

**Who can use it:** any member of your organization can use Friday. It acts as you, and only within your access rights - it can work with the tasks, documents, and people you can already reach, and nothing more.

**What Friday can do:**

- Read and edit documents - Studio pages and Word (.docx) files, including targeted rewrites, formatting changes, and insertions.
- Manage work items - create, update, move, comment on, and assign Tasket tasks across projects and sprints.
- Analyze and edit spreadsheets - read values, perform calculations, and save changes to live Studio spreadsheets.
- Search and look up - find org members, tasks, projects, and teams.
- Answer questions - using web search for current information, or its own knowledge for stable facts.
- Take multi-step actions - chain lookups, edits, and writes together in a single request.

## When to use

- A product manager needs to get up to speed on a task before a review. They ask Friday to pull the task and its documents together into a summary, instead of reading each item by hand.
- A team wants a shared piece of work the whole group can build on. They do it in a Team Session so everyone can see the conversation and the files it produces.
- An analyst has an exported data file to make sense of. They upload it and ask Friday to analyze it and build a spreadsheet.
- Someone needs an answer that spans several apps - for example, what was said to a customer across email and chat. They connect those apps and ask Friday to look across them in one request.
- A team keeps doing the same job the same way. They capture it as a skill so Friday produces a consistent result each time.

## How to use

### How Friday gets its context

Friday works from four sources of context. Knowing these is the quickest way to get useful results.

- **Your Neo workspace.** Friday can read and edit Studio pages and .docx files, create and update tasks, read and write spreadsheets, and search people, tasks, projects, and teams.
- **What you attach.** Use **\+ (Insert)** in the composer, or type **@** inline, to attach Studio files, Tasks, Friday sessions, or an uploaded file. Attaching a past session lets Friday pick up where earlier work left off.
- **Connected apps.** Connectors give Friday tools in other systems - such as Jira, Confluence, HubSpot, GitHub, and Outlook - using your own credentials.
- **Skills.** A skill packages instructions and resources for a repeatable job, so Friday does it the same way every time.

If Friday cannot tell which task, document, or person you mean, it asks rather than guessing.

### Choose a model and set effort

You can pick the model for each message from the picker next to the send button. It defaults to **Opus 4.8 · High**. You can also set **Effort** or turn on **Enable thinking** there.

### Find your way around the guides

This introduction covers what Friday is and how it fits together. The rest of the Friday user guide covers each area in depth:

- **Sessions** - starting My and Team Sessions, adding context, working with artifacts, rating responses, and sharing.
- **Skills** - adding, writing, using, and organizing personal and team skills.
- **Connectors** - connecting apps, using them in a session, consent, and disconnecting or requesting a connector.

### Keep track of usage

Each user has a monthly Friday spend limit, shown under **profile menu > Settings > Friday > Usage**. Each session also shows **Conversation stats** - cache use, output tokens, and cost - at the bottom of the conversation. To raise a limit, contact your org admins.

### Friday compared with a generic AI chat

A general-purpose assistant sits outside your workspace and does not know your tasks, team, or documents. Friday runs inside Neo: it works directly with your tasks, documents, and spreadsheets, reaches other apps through connectors that use your own credentials, and saves what it produces as editable files you can keep working on - not as a transcript you have to copy out.

## FAQ

**What is Friday?** An AI agent built into Neo that reads your tasks, documents, and connected systems, takes action, and saves the result as editable work.

**How is it different from a general AI chatbot?** Friday works inside your Neo workspace with your own access, can act in connected apps, and saves output as real files rather than chat text.

**What can Friday see?** Only what you can see. It acts as you and stays within your access rights.

**Where do I start?** Open Friday from **Switch product** and start a session from **My Sessions** or **Team Sessions**. See the Sessions guide for the full workflow.

**How do I give Friday the right context?** Attach the task, document, or past session with **\+ (Insert)** or **@**, rather than describing it, so Friday works on the live item.

**How do I keep an eye on cost?** Check **Settings > Friday > Usage** for your monthly limit, and Conversation stats at the bottom of a session.
